Orelega is a heavy slab-serif font with a large “ear” on the lowercase “g”, which seems to perk up. Other glyphs have similarly large ball terminals. The font blends the time-tested dignity of Clarendon with playful features reminiscent of children’s picture books.

The word orelega is Esperanto for “large-eared”. The design and proportions of Orelega were based on Sagona Extra Bold by René Bieder, but it breaks from its influence in many ways, such as by lacking inner serif corners and gaining new letterforms like a fanciful “&” and a more contemporary “ß”.

Orelega supports a wide range of Latin and Cyrillic characters.

To contribute, see github.com/HaleyHalcyon/Orelega.
